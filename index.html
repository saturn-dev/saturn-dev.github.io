What is deepinmycloset?
deepinmycloset is a modern rep spreadsheet. Most spreadsheets still run on Google Sheets, which honestly feels outdated. This is built to be cleaner, and faster. And deepinmycloset has the best rep links for this style.
What is an agent?
It's a service that helps you buy items from China and get them shipped internationally. You give them the product link, they buy it, check it, and ship it to you.
What's the best agent?
KakoBuy! They offer over ¥3000 in shipping coupons when you sign up through our link. Compared to other agents, KakoBuy just does it better. Simple, reliable, and easy to use.
How long do sellers usually take to ship?
Most sellers ship in about 2–3 days, but always check if the item is a preorder. Some sellers ship overnight. Once it arrives at the warehouse, you'll get free QC photos of your item.
Can I return my item?
Most of the time, yeah. Some sellers don't accept returns on cheaper items, but KakoBuy will warn you before you buy. If you do return something, refunds usually take about 3–5 days.
